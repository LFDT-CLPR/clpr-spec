# Source Filtering

`archive/eap-v0.2-source/` holds the EAP v0.2 specification text that the CLIP-0001 rewrite started from. It is kept for reference only; the specification is the text outside `archive/`.

The copy was filtered before it was added to this repository:

- **Included:** the v0.2 specification and design documents, the options document, the design records (ADRs), the Hiero and QBFT verifier documents, and the three architecture diagrams.
- **Excluded:** verifier documents for ledgers outside the scope of v0.2, tooling and agent configuration files, and repository metadata.
- **Edited:** references to products, organizations and people, and references to non-public repositories, issues, branches and source files, were removed or replaced with generic wording. Technical content was not changed.

The diagrams are identical to those in `archive/main/`.
